NMN vs NR: Does It Matter Which I Buy?

Many products marketed as NAD+ supplements actually contain NR (Nicotinamide Riboside) rather than NMN. Both raise NAD+ levels, but through different pathways. NMN is one metabolic step closer to NAD+ and has a growing body of specific human clinical trial data. If you are specifically targeting the benefits documented in NMN human trials, ensure your product clearly states NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) on the label -- not NR.
