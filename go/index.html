What NMN actually does

NMN stands for nicotinamide mononucleotide. Your body uses it to make NAD+, a molecule every one of your cells needs for energy production and repair. The catch is that NAD+ levels fall as we age. The theory behind NMN is straightforward: give the body more of the raw material, and it can keep NAD+ topped up.

That is why NMN research clusters around energy metabolism, cellular repair, and healthy ageing. It is a precursor strategy, not a stimulant, so the effects people describe tend to be gradual rather than a jolt.
